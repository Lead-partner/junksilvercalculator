# Image credits

Checked 14 Nov 2026. All originals retrieved through the Wikimedia Commons API with `prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|extmetadata|size` and a descriptive JunkSilverCalculator User-Agent. Raw API responses are retained in `sources-ref/images/` and `static/img/credits.json`. Original photographs were each viewed before processing; normalized pairs and annotation overlays were also reviewed.

The photographs retain their real details and toning. Outside backgrounds are replaced with the site surface color, with matching coin diameters in each pair. Smithsonian/NNC photos are split into sides. The Kennedy photos receive tighter crops. Crops are illustrative, not physical diameter comparisons between denominations. Derivatives of CC BY-SA material retain the original share-alike license. Public-domain components remain public domain.

Some independently photographed coins were graded by a grading service; their Commons metadata attributes the photographs to the individual photographer, not NGC or PCGS. No NGC/PCGS photographs are used.

## Teaching diagrams

Formula and silver-content SVGs: original site artwork generated from `COINS`, `coinOzt` and `TROY_GRAMS` in `src/calc.js`; MIT (repository license). Mintmark overlays use actual Commons photographs, with source and license links in the page captions. The war-nickel location is confirmed by NGC article 999; the Roosevelt location by NGC article 12205; the Washington location by NGC Coin Explorer. A half-dollar mintmark annotation is omitted because the allowed textual sources fetched did not confirm its exact position; its guide uses silver-content teaching figures instead.
